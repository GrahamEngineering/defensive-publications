## 2. The Core Safety Paradox: Mr. Rogers vs. Dangerous Bad Actors

Matt asks the critical question:  
> *"What if a dangerous psycho joins in? Is that an issue? How do we balance open sovereign freedom with real-world human safety?"*

### The Axiom 1 & Axiom 2 Sovereign Boundary:
1. **Axiom 1 (DO NOT HURT OTHER PEOPLE):** Non-aggression is the foundational constitutional invariant of the road trip. Anyone who violates bodily autonomy, endangers children, or commits predatory acts instantly forfeits participation and is permanently ostracized.
2. **Decentralized Neighborhood Watch (Web of Trust):**
   - No central police state. Instead, participants use cryptographic **Peer Vouching (Web-of-Trust)**.
   - Newcomers travel in open public commons. To share close-quarter sleeping spaces (campers, private tents) or ride in shared vehicles, travelers must hold mutual vouches from trusted friends.
3. **Immediate Airgap & Emergency Coordination:** If a dangerous actor appears, the convoy uses local mesh alerts (Meshtastic / local Wi-Fi) to immediately isolate the threat and coordinate with local emergency services.
